Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Perklyn collects, uses, stores, and protects information when customers and businesses use our website, progressive web app, Android application, and loyalty services.

1. Who we are

Perklyn is a loyalty rewards platform that allows participating businesses to manage their loyalty programs and allows customers to collect points, view business-specific balances, and redeem rewards. In this policy, “Perklyn,” “we,” “our,” and “us” refer to the Perklyn service.

Each participating business controls its own customer relationships, rewards, and business-specific loyalty activity. Points are never combined into a global balance shared across businesses.

2. Information we collect

Account and profile information

  • Name, phone number, recovery email address, account role, and encrypted authentication credentials.
  • For business accounts, the owner name, business name, and information needed to administer the loyalty program.

Loyalty and transaction information

  • Business memberships, loyalty balances, points additions or adjustments, configured rewards, reward claims, redemption records, and transaction timestamps.
  • Optional transaction notes and purchase amounts entered by a business when points are issued.
  • Customer and business identifiers needed to keep each business relationship separate.

Card and device information

  • RFID card identifiers used to associate a physical loyalty card with an existing customer membership. Identifiers are hashed for lookup and masked in management screens.
  • NFC provisioning tokens and one-time point-claim information. Loyalty balances are not stored on physical cards.
  • Push-notification subscriptions, device or browser capabilities, and notification preferences when notifications are enabled.
  • Basic technical information supplied with network requests, such as browser type, operating system, and security or diagnostic logs.

Information we do not intentionally collect

Perklyn does not require precise location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings, advertising identifiers, or payment-card details for its core loyalty services. A business may optionally upload its own logo or media for its loyalty program.

3. How we use information

  • Create and secure customer and business accounts.
  • Connect customers to participating businesses and maintain independent balances.
  • Issue points, process reward redemptions, and show transaction history.
  • Activate, scan, block, replace, and validate RFID or NFC loyalty cards.
  • Send requested account, points, reward, and business notifications.
  • Prevent fraud, duplicate transactions, unauthorized card use, abuse, and negative balances.
  • Operate, troubleshoot, secure, and improve Perklyn.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations and enforce our terms.

4. RFID and NFC loyalty cards

Physical cards contain or present an identifier; they do not contain the customer's loyalty balance. When an authorized business scans a registered card, Perklyn resolves it only within that business and retrieves the associated membership from the backend.

Unregistered, blocked, disabled, or replaced cards are not accepted for loyalty transactions and do not reveal customer details. Card activation and reassignment are restricted to an authenticated owner of the relevant business.

5. When information is shared

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared only as needed with:

  • Participating businesses: A business can access customers, balances, card status, and transactions belonging to its own loyalty program.
  • Supabase: Provides authentication, database, and server-function infrastructure.
  • Vercel and web infrastructure providers: Host and deliver the Perklyn website and application.
  • Push delivery providers: Deliver notifications when a user chooses to enable them.
  • Legal and safety recipients: When disclosure is required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users, Perklyn, or others.

These providers process information only to deliver their services and are subject to their own security and privacy obligations.

6. Data retention and security

We retain account and loyalty information while an account or business relationship remains active and for a reasonable period afterward when needed for transaction records, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, legal compliance, or service recovery. Temporary NFC provisioning secrets expire automatically.

Perklyn uses authentication controls, encrypted network connections, business-level authorization, masked identifiers, transaction locks, and access restrictions designed to protect information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, but we work to prevent unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or loss.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Notification permissions can be changed from Perklyn settings or device/browser settings.
  • Customers may ask a participating business to correct loyalty-program information controlled by that business.
  • You may request access, correction, or deletion of your Perklyn account information, subject to legal and transaction-record requirements.

8. Children's privacy

Perklyn is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information without appropriate authorization, contact us so we can investigate and delete it where required.

9. International processing

Service providers may process information in countries other than the country where a user lives. Where required, we use appropriate measures designed to protect information during international processing.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Perklyn's features, providers, or legal obligations change. We will post the updated policy at this URL and revise the effective date. Material changes may also be communicated through the app or another appropriate channel.
