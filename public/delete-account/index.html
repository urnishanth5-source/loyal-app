Account & data controls

Delete your Perklyn account

This page explains how Perklyn customer and business users can request deletion of their account and associated data, including requests made without access to the installed app.

How to request account deletion

  1. Email urnishanth22@gmail.com, preferably from the recovery email registered to your Perklyn account.
  2. Use the subject Perklyn account deletion request.
  3. Include your account role (customer or business) and your registered phone number. Business owners should also include their business name.
  4. Perklyn will verify that you control the account. We will never ask you to send your password, an RFID card number, or an NFC token.
  5. After verification, we will begin deletion and normally complete the request within 30 days.
Request account deletion

If the button does not open your email application, manually send the request to urnishanth22@gmail.com.

Data deleted

After identity verification, deletion generally includes:

  • Perklyn authentication account and login credentials.
  • Name, recovery email, phone number, and profile information not required for retention.
  • Notification preferences and push-notification subscriptions.
  • Active RFID and NFC card associations linked to the deleted account.
  • Customer wallet state and other account settings not required for retained records.
  • For a business closure, editable business profile, campaigns, and active loyalty configuration where no retention obligation applies.

Data that may be retained

Limited records may be kept when reasonably required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, accounting, or dispute-resolution purposes:

  • Points transactions, reward redemptions, and audit records.
  • Records needed to protect another user's or business's rights and balances.
  • Security logs and evidence of consent, requests, or prior misuse.
  • Records required by tax, accounting, consumer-protection, or other applicable law.

Retained records are restricted to these purposes and kept only for the applicable legal or operational retention period, generally no longer than seven years where a longer period is legally required. Data not subject to retention is deleted or de-identified.

What happens after deletion

  • You will lose access to the deleted account.
  • Active physical cards connected to the account will no longer be usable for loyalty transactions.
  • Unused points and unredeemed rewards associated only with the deleted membership may become unavailable.
  • Account deletion cannot be undone after it is completed.

Deleting the Perklyn app from your device does not delete your account. You must submit a deletion request using the steps above.

Questions or corrections

If you only need to correct your account information or disconnect from a particular business, say so in your message. You do not need to delete your entire account for a correction request.
